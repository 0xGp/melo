Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: people who already shop at public companies. Job: understand that everyday spend can become fractional equity. Action: connect a wallet and deposit ETH for MELO shares. Proof: an illustrative spend→ticker blotter, not invented volume. Constraints: no live brokerage; demo numbers labeled; purple + black; Geist; black-hole hero + bento as specified.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first viewport is a gravity well of spending — a real ray-traced black hole holding the right half of the frame — with ownership copy sitting in the dark left. It refuses a fintech screenshot hero, a metric strip, and a three-step icon grid.

OWN-WORLD: Void black ground. Committed violet (the accretion disk, one full-bleed CTA field, hairline rings). Geist for display and body, Geist Mono only for tickers and the blotter. Rounded 12–16px plates with a single 1px white/10 edge. No gradient text. No kickers.

STORY: A first-time visitor learns that qualifying purchases map to the merchant’s listed equity, sees an honest illustrative ledger, and deposits for MELO.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL black hole, focus high-right on desktop and low-center on mobile. Left (top on mobile) holds the MELO wordmark, the line “Spend becomes ownership.”, one sentence of mechanism, primary pill “Connect and deposit”, secondary “Read the whitepaper”. Nav is a thin overlay. The hole is the picture; nothing else occupies its half.
