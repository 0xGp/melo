# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who already spend at public companies (grocery, retail, software, fuel) and want that spending to become ownership instead of disappearing as a receipt. Primary job: keep shopping as they do today, and leave with a portfolio of the companies they fund.

## Product Purpose

MELO is a spend-to-own protocol. Qualifying purchases at participating merchants convert into fractional equity of those same publicly traded companies. On this surface a visitor can also connect a wallet, deposit ETH into the Melo vault, and receive MELO share tokens in that wallet. Success is understanding the mechanism and holding MELO.

## Positioning

Rewards are not points, cashback, or a partner coupon. Each merchant maps to its listed equity; the spend itself is the allocation event. A neighboring loyalty product cannot truthfully claim the same mapping.

## Operating Context

Marketing site plus a prototype on-chain vault. Connecting a wallet and depositing ETH mints MELO (ERC-20) into the depositor’s wallet. Illustrative merchant ledgers (AAPL, COST, etc.) remain demonstrations. The vault is not a brokerage, not DTCC settlement, and not a registered security offering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed mechanism: spend at a merchant → protocol matches the ticker → fractional shares accrue.
- Confirmed this session: wallet connect; ETH deposit mints MELO share tokens into the connected wallet (1 ETH → 1,000 MELO). If no shared vault address is configured, the visitor deploys a vault from their own wallet and deposits into it.
- Illustrative holdings (AAPL, COST, AMZN, GOOGL, MSFT and sample dollar amounts) are prototype demonstration data, not audited volume.
- The v0 vault holds deposited ETH; it does not purchase listed stock.
- No live waitlist backend, no KYC, no confirmed merchant contracts.
- Undecided: geography, brokerage partner, regulatory wrapper, mainnet shared vault.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MELO.
- Mark: user-supplied arched m in pink (`#F9A2DB`).
- Voice: direct, protocol-plain. "Spend. Earn. Own."
- User-pinned visual constraint for this landing: purple and black; the identity mark is the pink m.
- Type: Geist / Geist Mono as installed in the app shell.

## Evidence on Hand

- Prototype landings at `app/prototypes/landing/` (Orbital, Editorial, Velocity) — source of product copy and mechanism, not of fabricated scale claims.
- No customer testimonials, press, audited volume, or waitlist counts. Do not invent "$2.1B", "8,400+ members", or similar as facts.

## Product Principles

- Show the mapping (receipt → ticker → share), do not claim a market that does not exist yet.
- Everyday spend stays everyday; the protocol is invisible at checkout.
- Illustrative data is labeled illustrative.
- Ownership language stays precise: fractional equity of companies the visitor already shops with.
- The surface asks the visitor to connect a wallet and deposit for MELO, not to open a brokerage account.
